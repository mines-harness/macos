# Contacts and shared groups

Open **Contacts** beside Usage to copy your friend ID, change your name or picture,
or add someone using their friend ID. Your Mines account is separate from your
provider accounts.

Use **Shared groups** beside Your library to create a group and invite contacts.
Invitations wait in the invitation inbox until accepted. You can decline an
invitation, leave a group, or revoke an invitation you sent.
Groups support up to 32 people and 12 AI mines.

Shared groups have AI replies off by default, so people can chat and save notes
without starting any agents. Turn on **Ask AI** for a message when you want the
group's mines to respond.

After joining, choose which of your mines to add. Each mine keeps its own workspace,
model and provider account. Group members see the shared conversation; joining
does not share your private library, previous private messages or credentials.

The group's host computer must stay online. Conversations use direct connections
between devices. If the host or a direct connection is unavailable, the group
waits until it can reconnect.
